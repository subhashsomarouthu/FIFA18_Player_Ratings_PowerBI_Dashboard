# ⚽ Sports & Gaming Business Insights Dashboard

A data-driven Power BI dashboard built to empower decision-making in the sports and gaming industry. This project provides business professionals and analysts with powerful visual insights into player attributes, nationalities, wage distributions, age-based ratings, and in-game performance metrics.

## 📊 Key Features

- **Player Performance Analysis**: Evaluate top-performing players based on finishing potential, age, and overall ratings.
- **Demographic Breakdown**: Analyze players by nationality, age, and wage groupings to discover recruitment and marketing opportunities.
- **In-Game Economy Insights**: Support pricing and promotional strategies by tracking trends in in-game items and player valuation.
- **Stakeholder Impact**: Insights contributed to:
  - Increased sponsorship engagement
  - Optimized team compositions
  - Enhanced investment decisions

## 🧠 Technologies Used

- **Power BI** – for interactive dashboards and storytelling with data  
- **Data Analysis** – Python/Excel (optional pre-processing)  
- **EDA** – for identifying trends and business opportunities  
- **Visual Communication** – dashboards crafted with clarity and purpose  

## 🧩 Business Outcomes

- Enabled **data-informed strategic decisions** for marketing, recruitment, and player development.
- Improved **pricing and promotional tactics** using player performance and value data.
- Gained a **competitive edge** in sponsorship and investment opportunities by showcasing quantifiable performance metrics.
